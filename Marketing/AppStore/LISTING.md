## Description

Top Off is a calm, tactile pouring puzzle.

Tap a bottle, tap another, and watch the liquid flow. Pour matching colours together until every bottle is full of a single colour, or empty. Simple to learn, satisfying to finish.

- Hand-tuned levels that build from gentle to genuinely tricky
- Hidden colours that reveal as you pour, for a fresh kind of challenge
- A new Daily Puzzle every day, with a streak to keep
- Earn up to three stars on every level
- Hints and an extra bottle when you are stuck
- Undo and restart, always free
- No timers and no pressure: take as long as you like
- Works completely offline
- Colour-blind friendly: turn on shapes for every colour
- Satisfying haptics and sound, each with its own switch

## App Review notes

- No account or login. The app works fully offline.
- The first release ships with no ads enabled. When rewarded ads are turned on, hints and the extra bottle are offered for a short video, are free during the first two levels, and are granted free if no ad is available.
- Consent for ads uses Google's User Messaging Platform when ads are enabled.
